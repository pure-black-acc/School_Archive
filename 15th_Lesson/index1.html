Orbital Strike Cannon

An Orbital Strike Cannon is a Minecraft build featured in Unstable Universe utilizing various mechanics to be able to drop hundreds of TNT onto a chosen location. The machine was designed by cubicmetre.

End of light mob farm

The end of light mob farm (EOL) involves 2 key components: update suppressors and light suppressors. It uses an update suppressor to prevent the portals from updating when the side obsidian blocks are removed.
